Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we handle your data.

Our Commitment

At KAIDO, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our AI reminder assistant on discord and Telegram.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide our reminder service:

  • Your phone number (for discord) or Telegram ID.
  • The content of the reminders you set.
  • The time and date for your reminders.
  • Voice notes sent for the purpose of creating reminders (processed and discarded after transcription).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Create and send reminder notifications to you.
  • Process natural language and voice commands for your tasks.
  • Improve the accuracy of our AI for your specific requests.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your data. All communications are encrypted, and your reminders are stored in a secure database. We use industry-standard protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared when required by law or to provide the core functionality of the service (e.g., through encrypted messaging APIs).

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can view your pending reminders or delete them at any time by sending simple commands to the KAIDO bot.

6. Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of our service after changes implies acceptance of the updated terms.
